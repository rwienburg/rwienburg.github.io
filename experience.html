The Live Experience

More than a setlist.
A complete show.

The S.O.D.S. don't just perform songs they build an evening. From the first groove to the final note, every show is structured to take the audience on a journey through one of rock's greatest catalogues.

See Upcoming Shows Book The Band
What Makes It Different

Built for the stage.
Driven by the music.

Most tribute acts play the songs. The S.O.D.S. build a show. The difference is in the structure three carefully sequenced sets that move through the Dire Straits catalogue with intention, building energy, atmosphere, and emotional weight as the night progresses.

The arrangements stay true to the originals while the performance brings something alive that a recording never can. This is music that was always meant to be heard live and that's exactly where The S.O.D.S. deliver it.

The Show Structure

Three sets. One complete journey.

Each set has a purpose. Each builds on the last. By the time the finale arrives, the room knows exactly why they came.

01 Set One

The Build

The show opens with groove and tone. Set One draws the audience in establishing the sound, setting the atmosphere, and signalling to the room that something worth paying attention to is happening on stage.

The energy is measured, the musicianship immediate. This is where the band earns the room before they own it.

Character
  • Groove-led arrangements
  • Atmospheric build
  • Fan favourites introduced
02 Set Two

The Payoff

Set Two is where the room comes alive. Recognisable hits, high audience engagement, and a show-within-a-show moment a full performance of Private Investigations that stops the room in its tracks.

This is the centrepiece of the evening. The songs everyone came to hear, delivered at full power.

Character
  • Iconic hits at full energy
  • Maximum audience engagement
  • Private Investigations feature
03 Set Three

The Finale

The final set is where the show reaches its peak. Long-form songs, extended passages, and the kind of musical depth that only comes from a band fully locked in and playing to an audience that's completely with them.

The room leaves buzzing. That's the goal and the result, every time.

Character
  • Long-form catalogue pieces
  • Emotional and musical peak
  • Strong closing impact
Flexible Formats

Built around your venue.

The S.O.D.S. offer three show formats to suit different venue requirements, event lengths, and audience sizes. Every format delivers the same quality the structure adapts to your night.

01

One Non-Stop Set

A single continuous performance no breaks, no gaps, just music. Ideal for events where flow and atmosphere need to stay uninterrupted. Works beautifully for festivals, background entertainment that builds, or tightly scheduled corporate events.

Festivals Corporate Events Continuous Flow
02

Two Sets

The most versatile format. Two full sets with a break between giving the audience time to breathe, talk, and order another drink, while the band resets and comes back stronger. Perfect for clubs, pubs, and venue nights.

Clubs & Venues Pub Nights Mid-Length Events
03

Three Sets

The full S.O.D.S. experience. Three structured sets The Build, The Payoff, and The Finale that take the audience on a complete journey through the catalogue. Designed for theatre nights, headline shows, and venues that want to give their audience a full evening.

Theatre Shows Headline Nights Full Evening

Not sure which format suits your event? Get in touch and we'll help you find the right fit. All formats are available with or without the optional acoustic set.

Optional Add-On

The Acoustic Set.

Available as an optional addition to any booking, the acoustic set strips the show back to its essentials. Just the songs, the instruments, and the room.

It's a different kind of power intimate, focused, and surprisingly effective in both smaller venues and festival environments. This is one of the key differentiators that sets The S.O.D.S. apart from other acts performing the same catalogue.
