The Band

Four musicians.
One catalogue.

The S.O.D.S. are a Sydney-based live act built around the music of Dire Straits performed with the energy, precision, and respect the catalogue demands.

Our Story

Built for the stage.
Driven by the music.

The S.O.D.S. came together in 2023 with a clear purpose to perform the music of Dire Straits the way it deserves to be heard. Live, loud, and with the groove intact.

From the very first show, the response was immediate. Sold-out theatre nights, festival main stages, and repeat bookings from venues who knew they'd found something that worked. The band don't just play the songs they build a show around them, structured across three sets that take audiences on a complete journey from first note to final chord.

This isn't a note-for-note recreation. It's four experienced musicians bringing their own energy and commitment to a catalogue that earns it and audiences who've seen the show more than once will tell you it hits differently every time.

The Lineup

Meet the band.

Four experienced performers. One tight, groove-driven live act.

01

Neville Kaye

Lead Vocals · Guitar

Neville Kaye is a Sydney-based singer-songwriter and guitarist, originally from Johannesburg, South Africa. Since picking up the guitar at 18, he has built a prolific independent career, releasing 16 albums across rock, jazz, and contemporary styles. His songwriting has earned international recognition, including the L.A. Music Award for Best Classic Rock Artist, and multiple Australian Songwriting Awards. In 2022 he took first place at the ASA Awards for his jazz composition Dance On A Summer Night. In 2025, Neville released his final solo album turning his full attention to the stage. As the frontman of The S.O.D.S., he brings one of rock's most iconic catalogues to life with authenticity, energy, and his own unmistakable voice.

02

Richie Wienburg

Drums · Backing Vocals

Richie Wienburg is a Sydney-based drummer and backing vocalist, known for his dynamic, feel-driven playing and instinctive musicality. Originally from South Africa, Richie picked up the drums at 19 and quickly immersed himself in the live music scene with Cape Town alternative rock band The Damn Pesky Demons. Entirely self-taught, he made an immediate impact, winning Best Drummer at a local battle-of-the-bands competition just four weeks after starting. The band performed regularly over four years, blending originals with covers from Smashing Pumpkins, Red Hot Chili Peppers, and Nirvana. After relocating to London, Richie stepped away from music for a number of years before returning behind the kit in 2022 as a founding member of Kodak Kids. He transitioned from an acoustic Pearl kit to a Roland electronic kit, bringing precision and versatility to his playing. Richie now drives the live show for The S.O.D.S. with tight groove, energy, and musical sensitivity, anchoring the band while adding his own edge to every performance.

03

Chris Perry

Bass

Solid, locked in, and precise. Chris holds the low end with authority the kind of bass playing that you feel as much as you hear, and that gives the band its characteristic weight and depth.

04

Michael Samuel

Guitar · Keyboards · Backing Vocals

The harmonic backbone of the band. Michael's guitar and keyboard work fills the space between Neville's lead and the rhythm section bringing texture, colour, and versatility to every song.
