The Band

Four musicians.
One catalogue.

The S.O.D.S. are a Sydney-based live act built around the music of Dire Straits performed with the energy, precision, and respect the catalogue demands.

Our Story

Built for the stage.
Driven by the music.

The S.O.D.S. came together in 2023 with a clear purpose to perform the music of Dire Straits the way it deserves to be heard. Live, loud, and with the groove intact.

From the very first show, the response was immediate. Sold-out theatre nights, festival main stages, and repeat bookings from venues who knew they'd found something that worked. The band don't just play the songs they build a show around them, structured across three sets that take audiences on a complete journey from first note to final chord.

This isn't a note-for-note recreation. It's four experienced musicians bringing their own energy and commitment to a catalogue that earns it and audiences who've seen the show more than once will tell you it hits differently every time.
